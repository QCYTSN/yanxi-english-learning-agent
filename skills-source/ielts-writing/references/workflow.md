# Mandatory writing workflow

## 1. Question analysis

Identify Task 1/Task 2, question type, required response components, limiting
words and likely off-topic traps. Do not automatically generate a complete
outline unless requested.

## 2. Independent writing

For an exam-style session, use approximately 20 minutes and at least 150 words
for Academic Task 1, or approximately 40 minutes and at least 250 words for Task
2. Do not show a model answer first.

## 3. Evidence-first review

Create a short evidence table for Task Achievement/Response, Coherence and
Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range and Accuracy. Only then estimate
scores.

## 4. Guided revision

Give no more than three priorities. Ask targeted questions that force the learner
to expand, reorganise or correct the draft. Wait for V2.

## 5. Compare versions

State what improved, what did not improve, and whether the estimated criterion
range changed. Do not reward superficial synonym replacement.

## 6. Final review

Provide:

- remaining high-impact errors;
- selected sentence-level corrections;
- reusable error tags;
- optional minimal-correction version;
- optional natural version;
- a target-band example only at the end.
