# IELTS Reading question-type policies

## True / False / Not Given

- TRUE: the proposition is supported by the passage.
- FALSE: the passage explicitly contradicts it.
- NOT GIVEN: the passage does not provide enough information to decide.
- Never use outside knowledge or a plausible inference.
- Check subject, scope, comparison, quantity, cause, time and modality.

## Yes / No / Not Given

Apply the same logic to the writer's views or claims. Separate a reported view
from the writer's own position.

## Multiple Choice

- Locate the evidence before comparing options.
- Explain every plausible distractor.
- Common traps: copied keywords with wrong logic, correct detail answering a
different question, reversed cause/effect, excessive scope, and half-true options.

## Matching Headings

Identify the paragraph's controlling purpose, not its most memorable detail.
Check opening transition, repeated semantic field, development and conclusion.

## Matching Information / Features

Separate names, claims, examples and chronology. Do not assume information is in
paragraph order unless the task guarantees it.

## Sentence / Summary / Table / Flow-chart Completion

Check:

1. word limit;
2. required word class;
3. singular/plural and agreement;
4. whether the answer must be copied exactly;
5. semantic fit in both the passage and completed sentence.

## Short Answer

Answer the exact wh-question, respect the word limit, and avoid adding
unnecessary words that may make an otherwise correct answer invalid.
