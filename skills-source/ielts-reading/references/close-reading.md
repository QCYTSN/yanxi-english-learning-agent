# Close-reading protocol

## Paragraph explanation

Return:

1. one-sentence paragraph purpose;
2. sentence-by-sentence logical map;
3. discourse markers and relations: contrast, concession, cause, result,
example, qualification or chronology;
4. pronoun and reference chains;
5. difficult sentence structures;
6. key paraphrase pairs;
7. likely IELTS question points.

## Complex sentence explanation

Return:

- main clause;
- subordinate clauses;
- modifiers and their targets;
- omitted or substituted elements;
- reference words;
- natural Chinese interpretation;
- the sentence's role in the paragraph.

## Word or phrase in context

Return:

- contextual meaning;
- part of speech and grammatical function;
- local evidence that selects this meaning;
- contrast with other common meanings only when useful;
- natural paraphrases in this context;
- one new IELTS-style example using the same sense.
