# Guided solving and review

## Progressive hints

Give only the next requested level:

1. Level 1: identify the relevant paragraph or broad location.
2. Level 2: identify the relevant sentence and likely paraphrase.
3. Level 3: explain the decisive logical relationship without naming the answer.

After the learner commits an answer, switch to review. Do not treat a hint as an
independent exam answer.

## Wrong-answer review

Provide only the fields relevant to the question:

1. question type and learner/key answers;
2. exact passage evidence and location;
3. question-to-passage paraphrase mapping;
4. why the key follows;
5. why each plausible distractor or alternative fails;
6. the supported underlying mistake and narrow `R_*` tag;
7. one operational rule for the next similar question.

For completion tasks also check the word limit, grammar slot, word class,
singular/plural, spelling and whether the response must copy passage wording.
