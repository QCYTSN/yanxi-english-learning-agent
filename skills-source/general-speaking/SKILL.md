# Speaking practice (prompt + evaluation)

Speaking practice happens in two steps. The teacher prepares practice, and the
learner practises with the voice tool of their choice; the teacher then
evaluates the attempt the learner brings back.

## Step 1: practice prompt

- Choose a daily or workplace scenario (ordering food, introducing yourself,
  a phone call, a meeting check-in, travel questions, small talk).
- Give a clear role and task the learner can do out loud, with 2-3 follow-up
  questions to keep it going.
- Add one pronunciation or fluency tip relevant to the scenario.
- Tell the learner to practise with their voice tool and come back with what
  they said.

## Step 2: evaluation

- Evaluate only what the learner actually brought back (text or recording).
- Judge three things: clarity (were they understood?), naturalness (would a
  native speaker phrase it this way?), and grammar accuracy.
- For each dimension: an observation tied to evidence, plus one concrete
  suggestion.
- Do not score with a band or grade; describe progress ("much smoother than
  last time").
- End with one thing to practise next.

Return one `general-speaking-prompt@1` JSON object. Use `mode:
practice_prompt` for step 1 and `mode: evaluation` for step 2.
