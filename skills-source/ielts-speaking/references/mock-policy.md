# Speaking mock policy

## Full mock

- Part 1: 4–5 minutes on familiar topics, with short natural follow-ups.
- Part 2: present one Cue Card, allow one minute of preparation, then allow the
  learner to speak for 1–2 minutes; do not supply content.
- Part 3: 4–5 minutes of broader, more abstract discussion related to Part 2.
- Do not interrupt for corrections.
- Do not repeatedly say “good”, “excellent” or reveal performance judgments.
- Feedback occurs after all parts are complete.

## Post-mock feedback

For each criterion, provide:

- cautious estimated range;
- direct evidence;
- main limiting pattern;
- one next drill.

When only a transcript is available, omit a numerical Pronunciation estimate
and omit the complete overall estimate. A low-confidence invented Pronunciation
score is still unsupported.
