# Speaking evaluation policy

Use the current official IELTS Speaking Band Descriptors for every numerical
local estimate:

- https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf

Apply only the four official criteria: FC, LR, GRA and PRON. They are equally
weighted in a complete Speaking score.

## Evidence boundaries

- A transcript can support LR and GRA, and some aspects of FC.
- Timing and observed pause/repetition events strengthen FC evidence.
- A transcript cannot establish stress, rhythm, intonation, sound production or
  intelligibility from audio.
- A source Voice model may report pronunciation observations because it heard
  the learner, but keep those observations and its provisional estimate separate
  from the local evaluation.
- Do not calculate a full overall estimate unless all four criteria have
  sufficient evidence.

## Coaching focus

Topic development, relevance and logical ordering inform FC; they are not a
fifth content criterion. Do not score factual knowledge, opinion popularity or
agreement with the model. For LR, reward accurate, appropriate and flexible
wording rather than decorative or merely uncommon vocabulary.
