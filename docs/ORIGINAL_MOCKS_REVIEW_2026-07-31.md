## Public release boundary

These packs are local development material and are deliberately excluded from
the public repository distributions, wheel and Windows installer. A public
installation starts with an empty question bank. If a developer regenerates
the packs for private testing, they must remain visibly labelled
`practice_only`. Reading scores may report
raw accuracy; a Band estimate still requires a separately documented conversion
source. AI Writing and Speaking scores remain training estimates with
confidence labels, never official examiner scores.

No model answer is bundled before the learner writes. Reading answer keys stay
locked until submission, and Speaking mocks must remain uninterrupted.
