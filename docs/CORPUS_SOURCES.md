# Corpus sources

This project does not provide third-party test books or commercial question
banks. Users should acquire materials lawfully and import only what they are
permitted to use.

## Recommended official starting points

- IELTS preparation resources:
  https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources
- IELTS Academic sample test questions:
  https://www.ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test
- British Council IELTS preparation:
  https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/prepare
- IDP IELTS preparation:
  https://ielts.idp.com/prepare

Use these pages as external sources. Do not assume that free access grants a
right to repackage the contents under this project's license.

## Licensed private materials

Suitable sources include:

- legitimately purchased Cambridge IELTS books and digital resources
- legitimate e-books
- school or public-library access
- licensed institutional or course resources

The project intentionally does not include download links to unauthorised copies.

## Seasonal recalled questions

Seasonal speaking/writing recall collections are not official. When imported,
mark them:

```yaml
source_type: seasonal_reported
authenticity: unverified
```

Do not treat model answers supplied by third-party institutions as official
examiner samples.

## Project-original and synthetic material

Project-original questions can be openly shared under their declared license.
AI-generated questions must be marked `synthetic` and `not_authentic`, then
reviewed before use.
